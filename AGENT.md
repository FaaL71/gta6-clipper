# GTA 6 Clipper — what was made, and how

## The clip

`clip.mp4` — 22 seconds, 1080x1920 vertical (9:16), H.264 + AAC, 5.8 MB.

Source: Rockstar's official **GTA 6 Launch Trailer**
(https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rz1B4B2yBZY, 15 Oct 2026, captured on PS5).
I downloaded it with yt-dlp in this session and cut the action stretch at
**47–59 seconds** of the trailer.

## Why this moment (the creative decision)

The 47–59s stretch packs three pure-spectacle GTA 6 beats into 12 seconds,
with no dialogue to miss: a sports car tearing through a Vice City street, a
motorcycle frozen mid-air in a jump over palm trees, a night explosion, and a
biplane trailing pink smoke over a crowded beach. All instantly recognizable
as GTA 6, all movement and colour. The motorcycle jump lands ~3 seconds in —
right when a scroller decides whether to stay. I open on the street scene so
the first frame is already fast. The game released 19 Nov 2026, three days
before this clip was made, so launch-trailer footage is exactly what people
are searching for right now.

I first tried a centre crop to vertical, but the rider sits at the left edge
of the frame and the crop cut him off. So I switched to a blurred-fill
vertical: the full 16:9 frame stays visible in the middle band, with a
darkened blurred copy filling the top and bottom. Nothing is cut off.

## The pitch

The clip ends with a 10-second end card (dark purple, bold white text) that
reads:

"GTA 6 IS HERE" is overlaid on the first 3 seconds of the action. The
trailer's own music runs under the action and fades out under the end card.
